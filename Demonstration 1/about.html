About this project

Electrofy is a website built for the Demonstration 1 of the COS30045 - Data Visualisation course. It explores how much electricity household appliances use in Australia, starting with televisions.

Data source

The data story and the calculator use the Australian Energy Rating product register for televisions. Each model's screen size, screen technology, star rating and labelled yearly energy use are taken from that register.

  • Models

    5,340

    TVs on the register extract dated 3 October 2026.

  • Electricity price

    33¢ per kWh

    Used to turn each model's labelled energy use into a running cost.

  • Daily use

    10 hours

    Viewing time per day, the same basis as the Energy Rating Label.

Registration years are estimated from each label's expiry date. The data describes TV models, not households, so it contains no personal information.
