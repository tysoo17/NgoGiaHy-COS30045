About this project

The Appliance Energy Consumption Website is a small project for COS30045 Data Visualisation at Swinburne University of Technology. It explores how much electricity household appliances use in Australia, starting with televisions.

Data source

The data story and the calculator use the Australian Energy Rating product register for televisions (extract dated 3 October 2026, 5,340 registered models). Each model's screen size, screen technology, star rating and labelled yearly energy use are taken from that register.

Running costs assume 33 cents per kWh and 10 hours of use a day. Registration years are estimated from each label's expiry date. The data describes TV models, not households, so it contains no personal information.
